How SiteName matches you to a suburb

An explanation of the scoring, the data behind it, and how we handle your information, so you can trust the match before you trust us with your data.

The matching process

  1. You set priorities

    Rank affordability, transport, safety, and parks and recreation, then rate how much each one matters from 1 to 5.

  2. We score every suburb

    Each suburb gets a 1 to 10 score for each factor, based on public data. Some scores are still sample values while datasets are connected (see below).

  3. Weighted match %

    Your ratings become weights. A suburb's fit is its scores averaged using your weights, shown as a percentage of the best possible.

  4. You see the ranked list

    Suburbs appear best fit first, on a list and a map. Nothing is hidden, and you can change your priorities at any time.

We don't decide which suburbs are "good" or "bad". Only your priorities set what counts, so the ranking reflects what matters to you.

What we collect and why

  • Your quiz answers (priorities and weights) are used only to generate your suburb matches.
  • In this prototype they are stored only in your own browser. They are not sent to a server, sold, or shared with landlords, agents or advertisers.
  • Saved suburbs and saved listings are stored in your browser so you can return to your results.
