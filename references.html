References and credits

Everything on this site that comes from outside our team, and where to find it.

Data

  • Brisbane City Council. Park – Locations [open dataset]. Brisbane City Council Open Data. data.brisbane.qld.gov.au/explore/dataset/park-locations. Used live to calculate the Parks & Recreation score.
  • Opendatasoft. Explore API v2.1 (how the Council serves its data). data.brisbane.qld.gov.au/api-console/explore/v2.1.
  • Queensland Police Service. (2026). Reported offences rates by division [Data set]. Queensland Government. open-crime-data.s3-ap-southeast-2.amazonaws.com/Crime Statistics/division_Reported_Offences_Rates.csv. Offences against the person and property per 100,000 people over the latest 12 months, used for the Safety score (tools/build-suburbs.py).
  • Queensland Police Service. QPS divisions [Data set]. Queensland Government. data.qld.gov.au/dataset/qps-divisions. Police division boundaries, used to find which division each suburb is in.
  • Geoscape Australia (formerly PSMA). Queensland suburb and locality boundaries (2017), converted to GeoJSON by tonywr71. github.com/tonywr71/GeoJson-Data. Used for each suburb's centre point and area, and to count the bus stops inside it.
  • Proctor, M. Australian Postcodes [dataset]. github.com/matthewproctor/australianpostcodes. Used for suburb postcodes.
  • Australian Bureau of Statistics. (2022). 2021 Census of Population and Housing: General Community Profile DataPack, Suburbs and Localities, Queensland (tables G01 and G02) [Data set]. ABS. abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/datapacks. Licence: CC BY 4.0. Median weekly rent (Affordability score), population, age, birthplace, household size, household income and mortgage repayments on suburb pages (tools/build-suburbs.py).
  • Affordability, transport and safety scores are ranks across the 180 Brisbane City suburbs, calculated by our team from the datasets on this page.
  • kanchana1990. Australian Rental Market Data 2026 [dataset]. Kaggle. kaggle.com/datasets/kanchana1990/australian-rental-market-data-2026. Licence: add the licence shown on the Kaggle page. A downloaded snapshot (not live), filtered to Queensland listings by tools/convert-rentals.py. Used for rental listings, map pins and the median asking rent on suburb pages.
  • Queensland SEQ GTFS data is used to filter through and mark stop locations, in future this dataset will also be used to find routes and locations. data.brisbane.qld.gov.au//dataset/general-transit-feed-specification-gtfs-translink/resource/e43b6b9f-fc2b-4630-a7c9-86dd5483552b
  • Brisbane City Council. Suburb Boundaries [open dataset]. Brisbane City Council Open Data. data.brisbane.qld.gov.au/explore/dataset/suburb-boundaries. Used live to calculate the boundary shape.
  • Opendatasoft. Explore API v2.1 (how the Council serves its data).

Code and libraries

  • Leaflet 1.9.4, an open-source JavaScript map library, loaded from cdnjs. leafletjs.com · cdnjs.com/libraries/leaflet. Used on the "Browse suburbs" page (js/results.js).
  • MDN Web Docs. HTML Drag and Drop API, the pattern used to reorder priorities in the quiz (js/quiz.js). developer.mozilla.org.
  • Haversine formula for straight-line distances (js/app.js).
